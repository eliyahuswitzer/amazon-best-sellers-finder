# Privacy Policy — Amazon Best Sellers Finder

_Last updated: April 10, 2026_

Amazon Best Sellers Finder ("the extension") is a Chrome browser extension
that adds a floating button to Amazon.com search and product pages, linking
to the matching Amazon Best Sellers list for the category you're browsing.

This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

## What the extension accesses

The extension only runs on pages under `amazon.com`. When you visit an
Amazon search or product page, it reads the page content locally in your
browser in order to:

- Detect the category of the products you're viewing (by reading Amazon's
  own "Best Seller" badges and "Best Sellers Rank" information).
- Build a link to the corresponding Amazon Best Sellers page.
- Display a floating button that takes you to that page.

To improve category detection, the extension may fetch a small number of
additional Amazon product pages in the background from `amazon.com`. These
requests go directly to Amazon and nowhere else.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
- It does **not** track your browsing history.
- It does **not** send any data to the developer or to any third-party
  server. There is no analytics service, no telemetry, and no backend.
- It does **not** modify the pages you visit beyond adding its floating
  button.
- It does **not** run on any website other than `amazon.com`.

## Affiliate links

The links generated by this extension include an Amazon Associates tracking
tag. As an Amazon Associate, the developer earns a commission from
qualifying purchases made after clicking those links, at no extra cost to
you. Using the extension is free and does not require an account.

## Data shared with Amazon

Because the extension fetches pages from `amazon.com`, Amazon may log those
requests the same way it logs any other visit to its site, subject to
Amazon's own privacy policy. The extension developer does not receive or
see this data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same
URL, with a new "Last updated" date at the top.
